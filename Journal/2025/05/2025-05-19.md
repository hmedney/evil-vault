# Fighting Private Equity
The HOPE (Humans Over Private Equity) for Homeownership Act, led by Senator Jeff Merkley and Rep. Adam Smith, would penalize hedge funds that hoard single-family homes, often pushing families completely out of the market.

The HOPE for Homeownership Act would slam a 15% tax penalty on new hedge fund home purchases and eliminate tax breaks like depreciation write-offs. If hedge funds don’t start selling homes back to families, they’ll face $5,000 penalties per home.
